The **Maintainer** is **Koray Taylan Davgana** (the current copyright holder and
project maintainer of Makina). *(If maintenance moves to a company or foundation,
the Maintainer's rights and obligations under this CLA may be assigned to that
successor entity.)*

There are two versions below. Individuals sign the **Individual CLA (ICLA)**.
Contributors who contribute on behalf of an employer or other organization should
have that organization execute the **Corporate CLA (CCLA)**.

You accept and agree to these terms for your present and future contributions
submitted to Makina. The CLA-assistant bot records your acceptance on your first
pull request.

### How to sign

- **Individuals:** comment on your pull request with the exact sign-off phrase the
  CLA-assistant bot requests (configured in `.github/workflows/cla.yml`). The bot
  records your GitHub identity and the commit of this document you agreed to.
- **Corporations:** contact the Maintainer to execute the Corporate CLA and
  register your authorized contributors before they submit Contributions.
